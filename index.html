Who We Are

Bridging The Soil To The City.

"GoFresh is a platform built to make locally sourced food easier to discover, buy and enjoy while creating better opportunities for farmers."

For generations, agricultural distribution in Nigeria was tangled in costly broker networks, unnecessary storage delays, and price inflation. Farmers earned pennies while consumers paid top dollar for wilted produce.

GoFresh rewrote the equation. We built a direct, transparent digital marketplace where local agricultural growers post directly from the field, and families, restaurants, and grocers receive pristine food straight to their tables within 24 hours of harvest.
